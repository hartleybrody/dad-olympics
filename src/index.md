<section class="section wrap" id="events">
  <p class="eyebrow">Four Official Events</p>
  <h2>The Games</h2>
  <ol class="events">
    <li class="event">
      <span class="event-num">01</span>
      <h3>The Mile</h3>
      <p>
        Race with your oldest child in a stroller, wagon, or whatever they can safely ride on.
        Roughly 1.2 miles on quiet neighborhood roads. Keep them safe and close.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li class="event">
      <span class="event-num">02</span>
      <h3>Run &amp; Carry</h3>
      <p>
        About 300 yards, out and back. On the way out your child rides, pedals, or drives the
        vehicle of their choice. On the way back, dad carries the vehicle. The child may run
        back or be carried.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li class="event">
      <span class="event-num">03</span>
      <h3>Overhead Press &amp; Hold</h3>
      <p>
        Press your oldest child overhead with one or two hands and keep them above shoulder
        height, with no resting on your head or shoulder. Everyone starts together. Last child
        aloft wins.
      </p>
    </li>
    <li class="event">
      <span class="event-num">04</span>
      <h3>Entire Family Carry</h3>
      <p>
        Carry all of your children at once for a quick out-and-back across the lawn. You may set
        them down to adjust, but you cannot move unless everyone is off the ground.
      </p>
    </li>
  </ol>
  <div class="notice">
    <p>
      <strong>The Point Chase.</strong> The family carry runs in heats by number of children.
      Dads with more kids automatically place higher. This is not favoritism. It is arithmetic.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section wrap" id="what-to-bring">
  <p class="eyebrow">Come Prepared</p>
  <h2>What to Bring</h2>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li>Water and snacks, for parents and kids both.</li>
    <li>A folding camp chair or a blanket.</li>
    <li>
